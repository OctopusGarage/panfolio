## Central review corrections

- The salmon rice bowl is a serving idea with an incomplete sauce, not a complete new recipe.
- Pasta with the tomato stew is a serving accompaniment without supplied pasta cooking instructions; do not publish it as a fully independent child preparation.
- The instant-noodle egg custard uses broth rather than noodles; parent tags must not leak into every variant.
- The three tuna preparations and six rice-cooker meals need variant-scoped matching and explicit alternatives.
- Vegetable-forward recipes may contain meat, oyster sauce or chicken seasoning; a vegetable tag is not dietary certification.
- Basmati rice, short-grain cooked rice, tapioca sago, fresh alkaline noodles, steamed noodles and rice vermicelli retain separate ingredient identities and preparation requirements.
- Fulltext mentions inside counterexamples do not establish positive tags or applicability.
- Existing Chinese/Japanese/Dessert shelves are practical browsing shortcuts; source authors and actual cuisine/style should be represented independently in the proposed model.
