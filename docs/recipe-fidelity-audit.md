# Recipe fidelity audit

Date: 2026-11-25. Scope: all 24 current recipe entries, both Chinese and English. This report audits source fidelity, not independent scientific or food-safety validation. Existing IDs and source URLs are unchanged.

## Coverage and conclusion

All 24 entries were compared with available evidence. This is **not a completed frame-by-frame review of every original video**. Four SAMUKICHI entries were compared against full original English caption tracks and descriptions. The first twenty were compared against complete GitBook Markdown transcriptions; their original video or recipe evidence remains incomplete. See the [per-entry original collection report](recipe-audit-original-collection.md) for all twenty source links and retrieval results.

Fourteen entries link to original YouTube videos. Ten older videos yielded no usable requested-language captions: five caption requests returned HTTP 429, four exposed no requested-language captions, and one was unavailable. Ten other entries originate from written Xiachufang recipes, rather than identified videos; all ten original pages returned verification challenges. No inaccessible footage or challenged page is counted as reviewed.

Eight original-collection entries received supported corrections or clarifications. The four SAMUKICHI entries have no material ingredient, timing or sequence contradiction against the caption/description evidence reviewed. Remaining source gaps are explicitly tracked below; this is not a blanket certification that every recipe is error-free.

- **02 Bacon:** the written source introduces bacon in the final combined stir-fry without explaining its prior cooking state. Confirm from footage before adding an asserted preparatory operation or exact duration.
- **03 Shrimp oil and return:** the transcription does not explain separating the oil before broth preparation, returning separately cooked shrimp, or the extra shrimp juices. The creator's description links to `https://pse.is/3yfkr6`; this redirects to the creator site at `https://www.jamestaste.com/blogs/姆士流簡單做/88786`, which currently serves a missing-page notice. No speculative repair was made.
- **04 / 07 Congtou:** confirm the actual aromatic from footage; the English now preserves the ambiguous market term.
- **05 Egg handling:** the source's wording about whites and yolks does not securely establish a separate bowl-separation operation. Do not add one without visual evidence.
- **08 Shrimp endpoint:** the source says to add shrimp and quickly stir-fry without a clear duration or cooked-through cue. This is an inherited gap; original video retrieval was unavailable.
- **10 Tomato preparation:** the initial egg/noodle state remains unclear in the transcription. No egg ratio, noodle precooking method or microwave wattage was invented.
- **11 Finishing steam:** the source says low-heat 虚蒸 for 2–3 minutes after the 8-minute high-steam stage. Its precise intended meaning needs the original recipe. Do not silently substitute heat-off resting.
- **16 Ribs:** source specifies a partly cooked boil and subsequent frying without a boil duration. Do not convert that into an invented time.
- **11–20:** matching the GitBook summary does not certify the inaccessible Xiachufang original.
