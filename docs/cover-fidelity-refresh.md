# Cover fidelity refresh

The user requested a source-based regeneration of the twenty-four recipe covers added after the original twenty-entry collection. The change replaces their earlier watercolor/gouache treatment with natural photographic reconstructions. It includes the first four SAMUKICHI recipes, the nine-video expansion, coconut sago and ten subsequent dish entries. The standalone vegetable-cutting video has no dish cover to regenerate.

## Acceptance criteria

Every refreshed cover must use an inspected reference from its own original video, preferably a cooked finished-dish frame. Original storyboard sheets are acceptable when media downloads are unavailable. Source thumbnails are fallback evidence, with interpretation recorded explicitly. Generated prior illustrations are never used as image references.

Preserve actual ingredients, cuts, portions, vessel, plating and viewing angle. Represent natural surface moisture, irregular browning and believable rice, noodle, vegetable and meat textures. Avoid stylized paper backgrounds, CGI appearance, glossy plastic surfaces and repeated perfect geometry. Remove captions, logos and people without adding garnish or changing the dish. Multi-preparation entries select an actual demonstrated version.

All outputs are inspected before integration. Originals remain outside version control; production JPEGs and generated responsive derivatives use the established 1536 × 1024 pipeline. The current visual standard is recorded in [Visual direction](visual-style.md). Earlier source documents retain their original cover notes as historical evidence.

These images are generated editorial reconstructions. Photographic rendering does not make them original video stills or independent evidence of recipe quantities.

## Reference selection and output review

All twenty-four generated outputs were inspected individually, followed by a central review of the complete set against selected original references. Source-present garnishes such as the salmon radish/shiso and steak accompaniments are retained rather than invented. Broccoli represents the demonstrated miso-cheese preparation; the tuna entry represents tuna eggs; the rice-cooker collection now represents the actual chestnut-and-pork-rib plate rather than an invented bowl. The coconut dessert retains its original patterned bowl. All source files and generated PNGs remain outside version control.
