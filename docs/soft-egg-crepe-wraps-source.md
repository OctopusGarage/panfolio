The recipe `soft-egg-crepe-wraps` draws on the post and sampled video frames with Chinese captions. The original Xiaohongshu URL is retained in provenance. A corresponding YouTube upload was not established.

The video measures one soup ladle of flour and two of water with the same ladle. It shows stirring with three chopsticks for about one minute to a yogurt-like texture, then adding an egg. The batch yields about three crepes. The egg spread on each flipped crepe is separate from the batter egg. The pan is preheated on medium, then reduced to low once the batter goes in. Preserve the rising rim before the first flip, the set egg before the second, and visible puffing near the end. The source supplies no ladle volume, weights, pan diameter or per-side cooking times.

The potato filling demonstrates rinsing surface starch for a crisp result. The existing potato julienne reference covers that operation and offers a hand-cutting method for this recipe. The video uses a shredder. Lettuce, ketchup and stir-fried potatoes are the demonstrated filling. Cooked meat and shrimp are serving variations.
