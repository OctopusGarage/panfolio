# Visual direction

The interface uses mist white, pale sage, muted green text and restrained borders. Selected buttons use a pale green fill instead of a saturated accent. Small transitions respect reduced-motion preferences.

Recipe covers are AI-generated watercolor/gouache food illustrations made for Panfolio. The selected direction keeps ingredients recognizable without the visual noise of video thumbnails or exaggerated cartoon characters. All image assets are 1536 × 1024 pixels (3:2), with one centered dish, consistent viewing angle, generous whitespace and no typography. Original generated PNG files remain outside the repository; optimized JPEG derivatives are bundled for the app and offline cache.

Both list cards and detail covers display the full illustration at 3:2 without stretching or the previous video-thumbnail crop. New recipe illustrations should follow the same dimensions and visual direction.
