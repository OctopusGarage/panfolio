Cooking explanations and counterexamples are collected in the tips; editorial provenance and missing-source details stay in this document. Chicken marinade quantities come from the original description. The captions explicitly specify a center temperature of at least 74°C and resting skin-side up for 3–5 minutes. The cold-pan method is deliberately distinguished from the preheated-pan salmon method.

Broccoli blanching uses 1 teaspoon salt and 40–60 seconds, but water volume is unspecified. The miso-cheese preparation specifies approximately 2–3 minutes in the oven without a temperature or mode; it then uses a culinary torch. Neither oven settings nor a torch substitute are invented.

The salmon rice bowl is a serving suggestion, not a complete soy-milk miso sauce recipe. No sauce ratio is supplied. Total salmon cooking time and temperature are unspecified.

Asparagus roll quantities initially came from the Bilibili description and were subsequently confirmed against the original creator description during the [full collection audit](recipe-fidelity-audit.md). The optional butter and salt are listed there without a corresponding addition point in the roll captions. The basic asparagus method and pork rolls remain separate preparations. The basic short steam lasts approximately 10 seconds; neither water volume nor lid use is specified.

The four covers are AI-generated watercolor/gouache illustrations based on each source video thumbnail, with comparison layouts, text and symbols removed. The salmon cover interprets the cooked result using the reviewed recipe and the thumbnail fillet shape.
