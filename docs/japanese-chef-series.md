# Japanese chef series

The `japanese-chef` collection groups four SAMUKICHI videos under `#japanese-chef`. Existing recipe IDs remain unchanged. New recipes use stable `samukichi-` IDs and the normal bilingual renderer, saved recipes, notes and cooking reader.

Chicken marinade quantities come from the original description. The captions explicitly specify a center temperature of at least 74°C and resting skin-side up for 3–5 minutes. The cold-pan method is deliberately distinguished from the preheated-pan salmon method.

Broccoli blanching uses 1 teaspoon salt and 40–60 seconds, but water volume is unspecified. The miso-cheese preparation specifies approximately 2–3 minutes in the oven without a temperature or mode; it then uses a culinary torch. Neither oven settings nor a torch substitute are invented.

The salmon rice bowl is a serving suggestion, not a complete soy-milk miso sauce recipe. No sauce ratio is supplied. Total salmon cooking time and temperature are unspecified.

Asparagus roll quantities come from the Bilibili description. The optional butter and salt are listed there without a corresponding addition point in the roll captions. The basic asparagus method and pork rolls remain separate preparations. The basic short steam lasts approximately 10 seconds; neither water volume nor lid use is specified.

No unreviewed thumbnail is presented as an original recipe illustration. The new entries use a restrained text-led treatment; existing watercolor artwork remains unchanged. Source media and workflow scratch files are kept outside version control.
