# Panfolio kitchen reference index

Ingredient handling, storage, knife work, heat and seasoning. Read each method’s scope and limits; do not generalize one dish’s conditions to all dishes.

- [Washing and cutting broccoli](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/wash-broccoli.md) — Before cooking broccoli, wash crown-down and divide at the stems into even florets to remove trapped debris and cook evenly. (broccoli, wash, Vegetables, Cleaning, Slicing)

- [Peeling and cutting asparagus](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/peel-asparagus.md) — Peel only the tough base of asparagus; cut diagonally for plain searing, or wrap before cutting for pork rolls, retaining the tender core. (asparagus, Vegetables, Peeling, Slicing)

- [Trimming and evening chicken thighs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/trim-chicken.md) — Before searing a whole thigh, trim tough sinew and open thick areas so thin and thick sections cook more evenly. (chicken, Chicken Thigh, Meat, Trimming, Even Thickness)

- [Drying food before searing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/dry-before-searing.md) — Before searing fish, meat or asparagus, dry the surface; check again after salting, marinating or poaching so moisture does not interfere with browning. (dry, sear, Salmon, Chicken Thigh, Asparagus, Shrimp, Beef, Chicken Breast, Vegetables, Meat, Seafood, Moisture, Browning)

- [Salting salmon ahead](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/salt-salmon.md) — For this salmon method, salt 10 minutes ahead and blot the released moisture for seasoning and browning. (salmon, salt, Seafood, Salting, Moisture)

- [Choosing a cold or preheated pan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/cold-or-hot-pan.md) — Start thighs cold over medium-low heat to release skin fat gradually; preheat for salmon and asparagus instead. (pan, heat, Chicken Thigh, Salmon, Asparagus, Vegetables, Meat, Seafood, Pan Temperature, Rendering)

- [Searing fish and meat: contact and turning](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/contact-and-turning.md) — Gently press fish or chicken skin against the pan at first, release once set, then wait for browning and release before following the dish’s turning sequence. (turn, stick, Salmon, Chicken Thigh, Beef, Meat, Seafood, Pan Contact, Turning, Sticking)

- [Blanching, chilling and draining broccoli](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/blanch-chill-drain.md) — Chill broccoli in ice water as soon as it turns vivid green, then spread and drain it to limit further softening and diluted seasoning. (blanch, broccoli, Vegetables, Blanching, Chilling, Draining)

- [Searing asparagus before a brief steam](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/sear-then-steam.md) — Sear asparagus over medium heat before a brief steam with a little water, combining surface aroma with a crisp-tender center. (asparagus, steam, Vegetables, Browning, Doneness)

- [Soy sauce aroma from a hot pan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/soy-sauce-pan.md) — Finish broccoli, asparagus, mushrooms or fried rice by briefly letting a little soy sauce touch the hot pan before mixing for a toasted aroma. (soy sauce, Broccoli, Asparagus, Mushrooms, mushroom, shiitake, Rice, Vegetables, Grains and noodles, grains, Seasoning, Aromatics)

- [When to add butter and how to baste](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/butter-timing.md) — Match butter to the dish and stage: melting off the heat, spooning over nearly cooked meat and initial browning require different timing. (butter, baste, Salmon, Asparagus, Chicken Thigh, Beef, Chicken Breast, Vegetables, Meat, Seafood, Basting, Finishing)

- [Building garlic aroma without burning it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/garlic-oil.md) — For a garlic base, warm garlic gently in oil before adding food; choose either the garlic-first or garlic-later fried-rice sequence. (garlic, oil, Broccoli, Asparagus, Onion, Rice, Vegetables, Grains and noodles, grains, Aromatics)

- [How to brown cauliflower in the oven](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/roast-cauliflower-browning.md) — Dry and lightly oil cauliflower, space it cut-side down and wait for deep browning before turning. (cauliflower, roast, Vegetables, Browning, Moisture, Spacing)

- [Keeping stir-fried noodles toasty rather than soggy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/yakisoba-moisture-order.md) — Warm and loosen noodles, brown them separately and cook fillings in sequence; evaporate excess moisture before saucing to avoid soggy clumps. (yakisoba, noodles, Grains and noodles, grains, Separation, Moisture, Browning, Sticking)

- [Release mushroom moisture before browning in oil](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/mushrooms-water-before-oil.md) — Start thick white mushrooms with a little water, evaporate the liquid, then brown in oil to avoid using too much oil at the start. (mushrooms, browning, mushroom, shiitake, Vegetables, Moisture)

- [Oil-packed tuna: keeping oil and timing the addition](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/canned-tuna-oil-and-heat.md) — Keep some oil with oil-packed tuna and add it near the end of hot dishes; fold into softly set eggs to avoid drying it out. (tuna, canned, oil, Seafood, Oil Retention, Heating)

- [Lifting shrimp veins and rinsing gently](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/devein-shrimp.md) — Check shrimp size and the back vein, lift gently through a shell gap, then rinse, drain and blot before frying. (shrimp, devein, Seafood, Deveining, Cleaning)

- [Caramelizing onions and loosening browned bits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/caramelize-onions.md) — Cook even slices until deeply colored and soft. Pause between stirring for pan contact and dissolve brown deposits with a little water before they burn. (onion, caramelize, deglaze, Vegetables, Caramelizing, Deglazing, Browning)

- [Storing caramelized onions in a thin layer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/store-caramelized-onions.md) — Flatten cooked caramelized onions in a sealed bag for refrigeration or freezing, then take the amount needed for a dish. (onion, storage, freeze, Vegetables, Portioning, Refrigeration, Freezing)

- [Measure chicken poaching water and center separately](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/chicken-poaching-temperature.md) — Measure water temperature to control gentle cooking and the thickest center to check doneness; the final brief sear browns the surface. (chicken breast, poaching, thermometer, Meat, Temperature, Doneness)

- [Loosen fried rice, then let it contact the pan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/loosen-and-sear-fried-rice.md) — Loosen short-grain rice with a little water, then alternate pan contact and mixing to cook the moisture off. (fried rice, rice, pan contact, Egg, Eggs, Grains and noodles, grains, Separation, Moisture, Browning)

- [Boiling, resting and checking sago pearls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/cook-sago-pearls.md) — The 1-minute boil is only the start: small pearls rest covered, while large pearls also need staged reboiling. Check the centers of both. (sago, tapioca, Doneness, Resting)

- [Rinsing, sweetening and refrigerating cooked sago](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/store-cooked-sago.md) — Keep cooked pearls separate from dairy and fruit: rinse until cool and free of surface starch, drain, mix with sugar, cover and refrigerate. (sago, storage, Cleaning, Separation, Refrigeration)

- [Basmati rice: count broth and fluff after resting](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/basmati-broth-liquid.md) — Count broth toward total liquid, rest covered after simmering and release steam gently. (basmati, broth, Rice, Grains and noodles, grains, Liquid Measurement, Resting, Steam Release)

- [Slicing onion along the fibers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/onion-along-fibers.md) — Identify the fibers running from root to tip, then slice along them for stir-fries or soups where shape retention matters. (onion, slicing, Vegetables, Knife Skills)

- [Fine carrot julienne from thin slices](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/carrot-fine-julienne.md) — Thinly slice, align and julienne carrots for salads and other dishes. (carrot, julienne, Vegetables, Knife Skills, Slicing)

- [Slicing potatoes into even fine strips](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/potato-julienne.md) — Keep the blade upright, spread slices flat in overlapping layers and cut strips with even, square cross-sections; rinse until the water is clear before cooking. (potato, julienne, knife skills, starch, potatoes, Vegetables, Slicing, Starch Rinsing)

- [Fine bell pepper strips and green pepper slices](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/peppers-fine-slices.md) — Bell pepper strips and smaller green pepper slices use different blade movements. (pepper, slicing, Bell pepper, Green pepper, Vegetables, Knife Skills, Trimming)

- [Cooling and draining noodles before stir-frying](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/drain-noodles-before-stir-frying.md) — Cool and drain briefly boiled fresh alkaline noodles; cool and squeeze soaked Hsinchu vermicelli. Remove excess water before coating either with oil. (noodles, drain, Rice vermicelli, Grains and noodles, grains, Draining, Soaking, Moisture, Sticking)

- [Shrimp and eggs: drain the shrimp and mix eggs lightly](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/references/drain-shrimp-before-eggs.md) — Cook shrimp until nearly done and drain their oil and liquid.
