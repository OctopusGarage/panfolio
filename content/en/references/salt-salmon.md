# Salting salmon ahead

For this salmon method, salt 10 minutes ahead and blot the released moisture for seasoning and browning.

## Scope

Preparation for this pan-seared salmon method.

## Steps

1. Salt 10 minutes before cooking.
2. Blot the surface dry again before putting the fish in the pan.

## Reasons

- Salting ahead gives seasoning time to penetrate and draws out excess moisture. Salting just before cooking tends to leave seasoning on the surface.

## Common mistakes

- Salting immediately before cooking tends to leave seasoning on the surface.

## Limits

The timing belongs to this salmon recipe and is not a universal salting time for fish or meat.
