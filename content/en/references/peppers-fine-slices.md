# Fine bell pepper strips and green pepper slices

Bell pepper strips and smaller green pepper slices use different blade movements.

## Scope

Bell peppers cut into fine or decorative strips, and smaller green peppers sliced for pizza, soup or salad. Choose the cutting motion for the intended result.

## Steps

1. Halve bell peppers lengthwise, break out the stems and seeds by hand, then quarter. Pare away inner pith; trim more deeply for thinner results or leave thicker flesh for more texture.
2. Hold the pepper securely with fingertips clear of the edge and use the blade tip in a pulling cut toward you to make fine strips. Brief soaking can crisp and curl the strips for decoration; drain afterward.
3. Press the stem of a smaller green pepper inward to separate the stem and seeds from the flesh. Hold lightly and slice with a downward pressing cut.

## Reasons

- Trimming the inner thickness changes the fineness and texture of the strips. A petty knife is useful for trimming the interior.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting equally fine strips without trimming inner pith and uneven flesh.
- Leaving fingertips in the pulling path of the knife or cutting before holding the pepper securely.
- Making the brief soak for decorative strips a compulsory step for every pepper slice.

## Limits

Green pepper slices suit pizza toppings, soups and salads. Brief soaking is for decorative bell pepper strips, not a preservation process.
