# Slicing onion along the fibers

Identify the fibers running from root to tip, then slice along them for stir-fries or soups where shape retention matters.

## Scope

Onion slices intended to retain shape and texture after heating.

## Steps

1. Halve the onion and remove the core. Identify the lines running from root to tip and slice along that direction rather than across those lines.
2. Curl the guiding fingers, lightly guiding the blade side with a knuckle to control thickness while keeping fingertips clear of the edge. Push the knife slightly forward to make slices of similar thickness.
3. Use the slices in stir-fries or soups. For raw use, a brief soak can improve texture; drain thoroughly afterward.

## Reasons

- Cutting along the fibers helps preserve shape and texture when heated, useful for stir-fries and soups.

## Common mistakes

- Cutting in a different direction will not give the same shape retention.

## Limits

Use this direction when retaining texture matters; brief soaking is a raw-texture treatment, not a storage method.
