# Peeling and cutting asparagus

Peel only the tough base of asparagus; cut diagonally for plain searing, or wrap before cutting for pork rolls, retaining the tender core.

## Scope

Asparagus for searing or wrapping in pork.

## Steps

1. Peel the tough outer layer at the base.
2. For searing, cut diagonally and pat dry.
3. For pork rolls, keep the spear whole, wrap it first and then cut into bite-sized pieces.

## Reasons

- The tough fibers are concentrated in the outer layer at the base. Peel them to retain the edible core. Peeling the whole spear weakens flavor, while discarding a long base wastes edible asparagus.
- Diagonal cuts expose more surface and cut through some tough fibers, helping heat reach the center. Ordinary equal-length chunks can leave the tips and thick bases cooking at different rates.

## Common mistakes

- Peeling the entire spear or routinely discarding a long base wastes edible asparagus.

## Limits

Distinguish tough skin from the tender core; rolls and plain seared asparagus use different cutting sequences.
