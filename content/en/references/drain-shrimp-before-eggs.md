# Shrimp and eggs: drain the shrimp and mix eggs lightly

Cook shrimp until nearly done and drain their oil and liquid. Briefly combine yolks and whites just before cooking, then fold and finish with residual heat.

## Scope

Remus’s separated-egg and quick-folding method for shrimp with silky eggs.

## Steps

1. Season shrimp with salt, pepper, starch and sesame oil. Stir-fry until nearly cooked through and drain oil and liquid in a strainer.
2. Mix a little light soy sauce into the yolks first. Just before cooking, add scallion, briefly combine with the whites and finally add the shrimp.
3. Replenish the wok oil, pour in the mixture and fold quickly. Turn off the heat as the eggs begin setting, continue with residual heat until the eggs and shrimp are cooked, then plate immediately.

## Reasons

- Shrimp release liquid after frying; draining reduces oil and water carried into the eggs.
- Eggs absorb oil, so replenish it before combining. The hot wok continues setting the eggs off heat; serving immediately reduces liquid released during standing.

## Common mistakes

- Adding shrimp together with their drained oil and liquid leaves excess liquid to handle during the final cooking.
- Thoroughly beating yolks and whites together at the start mixes this sequence with a different scrambled-egg method.
- Plating immediately upon turning off the heat without completing the eggs and shrimp in the hot wok, or leaving the finished dish in the wok.

## Limits

This sequence belongs to this separated-egg preparation; keep it distinct from recipes that beat whole eggs together first.
