# Fine carrot julienne from thin slices

Thinly slice, align and julienne carrots for salads and other dishes.

## Scope

Salads and other preparations calling for even fine carrot strips, made by slicing first and then cutting those slices into julienne.

## Steps

1. Peel and trim the carrot. Make a flat surface, place it flat-side down and cut thin slices.
2. Offset the slices sideways and stack neatly, then push the knife forward to cut fine strips. Curl the guiding hand and keep fingertips clear of the edge.
3. Optionally soak briefly in water for texture, then drain well.

## Reasons

- A flat base is more stable. Slice thickness determines the fineness of the strips, and neatly aligned slices help produce an even finish.

## Common mistakes

- Cutting while the carrot rests on a curved surface makes it harder to hold steady; establish a flat base first.
- Uneven slices or a disordered stack produce uneven strips.
- Turning a brief soak into prolonged soaking, or adding undrained strips to a salad.

## Limits

Use for salads and other dishes needing even fine strips. Soak only briefly and drain thoroughly before use.
