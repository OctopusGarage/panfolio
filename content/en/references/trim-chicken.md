# Trimming and evening chicken thighs

Before searing a whole thigh, trim tough sinew and open thick areas so thin and thick sections cook more evenly.

## Scope

Whole boneless, skin-on chicken thighs for pan-searing.

## Steps

1. Remove firm fat, sinew and cartilage.
2. Lay the thigh skin-side down. Make shallow cuts in thick flesh and open it out to even the thickness without cutting through the skin.
3. Pat both sides dry after trimming.

## Reasons

- Remove firm fat, sinew and cartilage but keep soft fat for flavor. Open thick areas from the flesh side to even the thickness, so thin areas do not overcook while thick areas remain underdone.

## Common mistakes

- Uneven thickness can leave thin areas overcooked and thick areas underdone.
- Removing all fat sacrifices some of its flavor.

## Limits

This method is for boneless, skin-on thighs, rather than a trimming rule for every cut of meat.
