# Washing and cutting broccoli

Before cooking broccoli, wash crown-down and divide at the stems into even florets to remove trapped debris and cook evenly.

## Scope

Broccoli being prepared for blanching, searing or grilling.

## Steps

1. Submerge the crown upside down in water and swirl gently; optionally cover with paper towels for a brief soak.
2. Cut into the branching stems between florets, then separate by hand. Divide larger florets further so the pieces are similar in size.
3. Trim the tough stalk base, peel coarse skin and cut the stalk for cooking.

## Reasons

- Debris can lodge between florets, so a quick rinse may not be enough. Submerge the crown upside down and swirl gently to loosen trapped particles.
- Uneven florets cook unevenly: smaller pieces soften before larger ones. Score the stems and separate by hand for similar sizes and a natural shape.
- The stalk is edible too. Trim the tough base and peel its coarse skin to reduce toughness and enjoy a different sweetness and texture from the florets.

## Common mistakes

- A quick rinse can leave debris between florets.
- Large differences in floret size lead to uneven cooking.

## Limits

This preparation is specific to broccoli; choose piece size for the dish you are making.
