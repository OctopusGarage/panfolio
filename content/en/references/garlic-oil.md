# Building garlic aroma without burning it

For a garlic base, warm garlic gently in oil before adding food; choose either the garlic-first or garlic-later fried-rice sequence.

## Scope

Garlic broccoli; asparagus pork rolls also use garlic oil. The garlic base for onion soup and the alternative garlic-first, low-heat fried-rice sequence.

## Steps

1. For garlic broccoli, put olive oil and garlic in the pan.
2. Heat slowly until fragrant, then add drained broccoli for searing.
3. When using prepared garlic oil for pork rolls, heat it until fragrant before adding the rolls.
4. For onion soup, gently heat sliced garlic in oil until fragrant without browning, then add water and chicken stock powder.
5. If choosing garlic-first fried rice, infuse it over low heat before adding egg and rice. The main method instead adds garlic after the rice dries; choose one sequence.

## Reasons

- For the garlic version, infuse the oil slowly before lightly browning the broccoli. A little soy sauce around the pan edge adds a roasted aroma; add black pepper off the heat to preserve its fragrance.

## Common mistakes

- Rushing straight to searing skips the gentle garlic-infusion stage.

## Limits

This is an immediate cooking technique, not a garlic-oil storage or long-term meal-preparation method.
