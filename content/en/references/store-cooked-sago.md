# Rinsing, sweetening and refrigerating cooked sago

Keep cooked pearls separate from dairy and fruit: rinse until cool and free of surface starch, drain, mix with sugar, cover and refrigerate.

## Scope

Fully cooked pearls being kept for later, before mixing with dairy and fruit.

## Steps

1. Rinse fully cooked pearls repeatedly in cool water until no longer sticky and the water is clear and cold, then drain.
2. Mix one large spoonful of granulated sugar into the cooked batch, cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate.
3. Preferably use within 3 days. Stir briefly after removing from the refrigerator to loosen the pearls, then assemble the dessert.

## Reasons

- Rinsing removes surface starch and reduces sticking.
- Cooked pearls mixed with sugar are easier to loosen after a night in the refrigerator; the purpose is separation.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping rinsing and draining, leaving sticky cooked pearls piled together.
- Treating sugar as a preservative or applying the pearl-storage period to the assembled dessert with dairy and fruit.

## Limits

The large spoonful belongs to the demonstrated batch, without a fixed dry-pearl weight ratio. Refrigeration guidance concerns the cooked pearls alone; sugar does not replace refrigeration.
