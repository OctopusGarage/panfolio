# Searing asparagus before a brief steam

Sear asparagus over medium heat before a brief steam with a little water, combining surface aroma with a crisp-tender center.

## Scope

Pan-searing diagonally cut asparagus.

## Steps

1. Dry the asparagus and add it to preheated oil.
2. Sear over medium heat, rolling occasionally, until slightly flexible rather than limp.
3. Before final seasoning, add a splash of water and steam for about 10 seconds, then season.

## Reasons

- High heat can scorch the outside while leaving a tough center. Use medium heat and roll occasionally for even cooking. Finish while slightly flexible rather than limp, allowing for residual heat.
- A brief steam helps heat the center and brightens the color. Searing first and steaming briefly combines aroma with a crisp-tender bite.

## Common mistakes

- High heat alone can scorch the outside while leaving a tough center.
- Cooking until completely limp sacrifices the crisp-tender texture.

## Limits

The brief steaming time belongs to this asparagus method, not other vegetables or meat.
