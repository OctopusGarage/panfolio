# Cooling and draining noodles before stir-frying

Cool and drain briefly boiled fresh alkaline noodles; cool and squeeze soaked Hsinchu vermicelli. Remove excess water before coating either with oil.

## Scope

Fresh alkaline noodles and Hsinchu rice vermicelli in these two home stir-fries, each with its own preparation.

## Steps

1. Parboil fresh alkaline noodles over high heat, adding cold water in two or three additions, for about 40 seconds until roughly seven-tenths cooked. Cool in cold water, drain thoroughly and coat with oil and dark soy sauce.
2. Soak Hsinchu rice vermicelli in warm water for 10 minutes, rinse cold, squeeze dry and toss with a little cooking oil.
3. Prepare the vegetables and eggs separately before combining with the noodles, keeping excess surface water out of the pan.

## Reasons

- Home burners have limited power. Excess surface water makes noodles harder to separate and dry quickly; draining and coating lightly with oil help reduce sticking.
- These noodle products hydrate and precook differently; brief boiling for fresh alkaline noodles is not interchangeable with warm soaking for vermicelli.

## Common mistakes

- Adding wet clumps after only a brief drain, or assigning the same cooking time to different noodle products.

## Limits

Timings concern these two noodle products. Steamed yakisoba, cold noodles and other rice noodles retain their own preparation methods.
