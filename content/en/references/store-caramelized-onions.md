# Storing caramelized onions in a thin layer

Flatten cooked caramelized onions in a sealed bag for refrigeration or freezing, then take the amount needed for a dish.

## Scope

Cooked caramelized onions intended for later soups, curries or sauces.

## Steps

1. Put cooked caramelized onions in a resealable storage bag, flatten into a thin layer and seal rather than packing the whole batch into a thick mass.
2. Refrigerate for 3～4 days or freeze for about 2～3 months. These are refrigerated or frozen storage options, not room-temperature holding periods.
3. Take the amount needed for a soup, curry or sauce instead of using the entire bag merely to obtain a small portion.

## Reasons

- Caramelized onions take time, so a larger batch reduces repeated work.
- A thin layer makes it easier to take the amount needed without using the entire bag.

## Common mistakes

- Packing the batch into a thick mass makes small portions harder to take.

## Limits

These storage periods apply to these caramelized onions, not to other cooked foods, soups or canned foods.
