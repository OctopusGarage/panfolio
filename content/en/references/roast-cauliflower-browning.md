# How to brown cauliflower in the oven

Dry and lightly oil cauliflower, space it cut-side down and wait for deep browning before turning.

## Scope

Cauliflower roasted in a home convection oven, including a cheese finish after the basic roast.

## Steps

1. Halve large florets to create a flat side; small florets may remain whole. Wash and dry thoroughly, including between the florets.
2. Lightly coat with garlic oil or olive oil until just shiny. For 300 g of cauliflower, about 2.2 g salt is a useful guideline.
3. Arrange in a single layer with space between florets and the flat sides firmly against the tray.
4. Fully preheat to 220°C (425°F). Roast for 12 minutes without turning; if the cut side is pale, add 1–2 minutes at a time.
5. Once deeply browned, turn just once and roast for about 5 minutes more. Adjust for the oven and floret size, checking browning on the cut surface and edges and tenderness inside.

## Reasons

- A flat side increases direct tray contact and browns more readily than a large whole floret.
- Surface water and steam trapped by crowding interfere with browning. A thin oil coating helps transfer heat; no oil can leave the surface dry yet pale.
- A fully preheated oven and uninterrupted tray contact give the cut surface time to brown.

## Common mistakes

- Roasting wet, overlapping or crowded florets steams them.
- Frequent turning, or turning while the cut side is pale, interrupts deep browning.

## Limits

These temperatures and times belong to this cauliflower preparation, not every vegetable. Add cheese after the basic roast and broil until melted.
