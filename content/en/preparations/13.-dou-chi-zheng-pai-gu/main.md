# Steamed pork ribs with black bean sauce

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 13.-dou-chi-zheng-pai-gu / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- 500 g pork ribs; 1 potato, sliced
- 2 spoonfuls minced garlic and half a spoonful fermented black beans
- 2 spoonfuls light soy sauce, 1 spoonful oyster sauce and 1 spoonful starch
- A little sugar and salt; cooking oil
- Flour and water, for cleaning the ribs

## Method

### 1. Clean the ribs

Rub and wash the ribs with flour and water for 2 minutes. Rinse with clean water and drain.

### 2. Make the black-bean sauce

Put the garlic and black beans in a bowl. Pour hot oil over them to release their aroma, then mix in the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar and salt.

### 3. Marinate the ribs

Add the sauce and starch to the ribs. Mix thoroughly and marinate for about 30 minutes.

### 4. Arrange on a plate

Line the steaming plate with potato slices and spread the ribs evenly over them.

### 5. Steam and serve

Once the water boils, steam over high heat for 25 minutes. Remove when cooked.
