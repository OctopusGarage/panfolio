# Shacha, peanut and milk noodles

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 10.-rang-pao-mian-hao-chi-fan-bei-de-10-zhong-zuo-fa / shacha-milk

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Method

### 1. Shacha, peanut and milk noodles

1. Fry 2 spoonfuls shacha sauce until fragrant.
2. Add boiling water, 1 small spoonful peanut butter and half a cup of milk.
3. Add the noodles and cook through. Add only a little seasoning powder to taste.

Tip: The shacha sauce, peanut butter and milk make a rich broth.
