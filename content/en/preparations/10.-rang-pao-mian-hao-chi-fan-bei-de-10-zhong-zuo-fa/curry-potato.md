# Curry and potato noodles

## Method

### 1. Curry and potato noodles

1. Pan-fry the potatoes until cooked through, then add a curry block and fry until fragrant.
2. Add hot water and cook the noodles in the broth.

Tip: Curry blocks are already seasoned, so the powder sachet can be omitted.
