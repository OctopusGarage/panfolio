## Method

### 1. Fried egg and ham spicy noodles

1. Pan-fry the egg and ham sausage until lightly browned and fragrant.
2. Add water and bring to a boil. Add the noodles and seasoning sachets and cook through.

Tip: Frying first adds egg and ham flavor to the broth.
