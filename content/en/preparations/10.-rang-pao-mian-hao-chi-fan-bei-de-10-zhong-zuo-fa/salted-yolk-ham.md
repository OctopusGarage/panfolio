# Salted egg yolk and ham noodle soup

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 10.-rang-pao-mian-hao-chi-fan-bei-de-10-zhong-zuo-fa / salted-yolk-ham

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Method

### 1. Salted egg yolk and ham noodle soup

1. Heat oil, fry the ginger until fragrant, then add salted egg yolk and cook until grainy.
2. Add ham sausage, hot water and the noodles.
3. Add vegetables such as Chinese cabbage or tofu if desired and cook through.

Tip: Salted egg yolk is already salty; use the seasoning powder sparingly.
