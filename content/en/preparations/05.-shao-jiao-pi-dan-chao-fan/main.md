# Charred pepper and century egg fried rice

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 05.-shao-jiao-pi-dan-chao-fan / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- 2 green chilies and 3 nuomijiao peppers (mild green peppers)
- Century eggs, eggs, cooked rice and chopped scallions
- Sichuan pepper oil, soy sauce or thick soy sauce, white pepper and sesame oil
- Salt and cooking oil

## Method

### 1. Char the peppers

Roast the peppers until the skins are blackened. Place in a bowl, cover with plastic wrap and let the skins soften, then peel. Leave a little charred skin for flavor if desired.

### 2. Make the century-egg relish

Peel and crush the century eggs. Mix with the peppers, scallions, Sichuan pepper oil, soy sauce, white pepper and sesame oil.

### 3. Fry the eggs and rice

Heat the pan, then add oil and crack in the eggs. Let them cook briefly to develop aroma before adding the rice. Break up the rice and fry off excess moisture.

### 4. Add the relish

Add scallions and about 1 spoonful of the century-egg relish. Stir-fry to combine.

### 5. Adjust the seasoning

Add a little soy sauce, salt and white pepper to taste. Toss and serve.

## Tips

- The relish also works with noodles or as a dipping sauce.
- For a stronger century-egg flavor, serve a little extra relish alongside the rice.
