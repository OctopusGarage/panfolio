# Lamb pilaf

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: magic-lamb-pilaf / lamb-pilaf

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Pilaf: 500 g bone-in lamb; 1 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon baking soda for marinating; half an onion; 2～3 carrots; 2 tablespoons oil; 1 teaspoon salt; 1 teaspoon coarse cumin; 1 teaspoon coarse ground coriander seed (optional); 1 cup water for braising; 500 ml basmati rice; broth plus water totaling 1000 ml; raisins.

## Method

### 1. Marinate lamb and cut vegetables

Marinate lamb chops, shoulder or leg with the marinating salt and baking soda for 15 minutes. Rinse away surface salt and baking soda and pat dry. Slice onion; cut carrots diagonally into slices, then lengthwise into strips.



### 2. Brown and braise

Fry onion in hot oil; as it begins to color, add lamb in a single layer over medium heat. Turn when the underside browns; remove when lamb and onion are golden brown. In the remaining oil and rendered lamb fat, fry carrots, salt, cumin and coriander seed until fragrant. Return lamb and onion, stir and add the braising water. Bring to a boil and simmer over low heat for 30 minutes, until chopsticks enter the meat easily.



### 3. Measure broth and cook pilaf

About 100 ml broth remains in the demonstration; measure it in a jug if unsure. Add water to reach the specified total liquid rather than adding a full separate portion of water. Combine meat, vegetables, rice and liquid in a tightly covered pot, level the rice and taste the broth for salt. Cover and bring to a boil, add raisins, cover again and simmer on low for 15 minutes. Rest off heat for 5 minutes, then uncover and turn gently to release steam. Rice should be plump without excess water at the bottom, and lamb tender enough to come off the bone.

## Tips

- The demonstrated basmati volume ratio is 1:2; check the package for other rice varieties and their cooking times. Unsoaked rice may need 1～2 minutes longer. No-wash basmati need not be washed; washing removes surface starch, but this rice is naturally loose.
- Freshly cooked long grains are fragile; turn gently to release steam rather than stirring repeatedly. Brown onion first so it later softens into the broth. The pilaf cooks longer than plain rice because it contains meat, vegetables and fat.
