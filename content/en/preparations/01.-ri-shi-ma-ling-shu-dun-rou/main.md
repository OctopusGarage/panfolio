# Japanese beef and potato stew (Nikujaga)

Japanese chef series · Preparation type: dish

ID: 01.-ri-shi-ma-ling-shu-dun-rou / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes, cut into large chunks
- Onion, sliced along the grain
- Beef short rib or thinly sliced beef
- Sugar, soy sauce, sake or mirin, cooking oil and water

## Method

### 1. Brown the onion

Heat a little oil in a pan. Add the onion and cook until the edges are lightly browned.

### 2. Cook the beef

Add the beef and stir-fry until no red remains on the surface and some fat has rendered. Add sugar and stir to combine.

### 3. Add the potatoes

Add the potatoes, soy sauce and sake or mirin. Pour in water until it is level with the ingredients.

### 4. Simmer under a drop lid

Cut a piece of baking paper or foil to fit the pan and lay it directly over the ingredients as a drop lid. Simmer over medium-low heat for about 25 minutes.

### 5. Reduce and serve

Cook until the beef and potatoes are tender and the sauce has reduced. Pierce a potato with chopsticks to check that it is cooked through, then serve.

## Tips

- Large potato chunks hold their shape better during simmering.
- A drop lid helps the sauce circulate and season the ingredients evenly.
- Serve with rice, pickles or stir-fried greens.
