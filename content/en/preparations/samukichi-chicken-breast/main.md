# Gently poached salt-and-sugar chicken breast

Japanese chef series · Preparation type: dish

ID: samukichi-chicken-breast / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- 300 g skin-on chicken breast
- 3 g salt, 1% of the meat weight
- 1.5 g sugar, 0.5% of the meat weight
- Butter as needed
- Water

## Method

### 1. Trim the tendons

Feel for firm tendon lines on the flesh side. Carefully cut them with the knife tip, trim bundled tendons and remaining tough sinew, then pierce the skin side with a fork to reduce curling. Pat the surface dry with paper towels.

### 2. Season with salt and sugar

Rub salt and sugar evenly over the breast and rest for 10～20 minutes. For bagged poaching, use a food bag explicitly labeled heat-resistant and suitable for cooking, remove the air and seal. Alternatively, poach the seasoned chicken directly in the water without a bag.

### 3. Poach gently

Bring water to a boil, turn off the heat and use a thermometer to confirm it has cooled to 80～85°C before adding the chicken. With a bag, keep the meat submerged and prevent the bag from touching the bottom or sides of the pot. Use 23～25 minutes as the first checking point for this breast; water temperature, thickness and starting temperature affect the result.

### 4. Check the center temperature

Measure the center at the thickest part and confirm at least 74°C (165°F). If lower, reheat the water to 80～85°C, poach for a few more minutes and check again until the center reaches the target. Water temperature is not center temperature. Time, color and touch alone cannot establish doneness.

### 5. Briefly brown the surface

Remove the chicken and pat dry. Add butter to a pan and briefly sear over medium heat, skin side first, then turn and brown the other side. Remove and slice once colored. This stage develops surface flavor; prolonged searing should not substitute for checking the center earlier.

## Tips

- Fierce direct searing of a thick breast can brown the outside before the center cooks, while prolonged rolling boiling can make it firm. Separate gentle center cooking from final browning to control each stage.
- Pat the trimmed raw chicken dry directly with paper towels, avoiding splashes from washing. Clean hands, tools and surfaces that contact raw chicken promptly to prevent cross-contamination.
- The video waits 30 seconds after boiling and switching off the heat, but the pot, water quantity and surroundings affect cooling. Do not assume this always produces 80～85°C water; measure it.
- Rub the salt and sugar evenly over the meat first. A bag reduces seasoning loss; the bag-free version also starts with seasoning. Ordinary resealable bags are not automatically suitable for heated cooking.
- The center check of at least 74°C (165°F) also follows USDA poultry safety guidance. Even water maintained at 80～85°C does not replace measuring the center.
- This brief salt-and-sugar treatment differs from acid marinating or enzyme tenderising. Do not add acid or tenderising powder, or greatly extend the marinating time, in pursuit of more tenderness. Refrigerate during marinating and continue controlling water temperature and checking the centre separately.
