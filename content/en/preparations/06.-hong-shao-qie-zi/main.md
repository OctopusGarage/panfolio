# Braised eggplant

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 06.-hong-shao-qie-zi / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Several purple eggplants and some lean meat
- Qinglong green peppers and garlic
- Salt, white vinegar, Suanrongxian garlic seasoning, pepper and sugar
- Dark soy sauce, light soy sauce and cooking oil
- Cornstarch and water

## Method

### 1. Prepare the eggplant

Wash the eggplant in lightly salted water. Remove the stems and cut into rolling-cut chunks. Mix with salt and white vinegar, leave for 10 minutes, rinse off the salt and squeeze out as much water as possible.

### 2. Prepare the other ingredients

Mince the meat. Slice the green peppers diagonally and crush and mince the garlic.

### 3. Mix the sauce

Combine salt, garlic seasoning, pepper, sugar, dark soy sauce, light soy sauce and oil. Separately mix cornstarch with water to make a slurry.

### 4. Cook the eggplant

Heat oil in a pan. Stir-fry the eggplant until softened and changed in color, then remove.

### 5. Cook the meat

Leave a little oil in the pan. Add the minced meat and garlic and stir-fry until the meat changes color and becomes fragrant.

### 6. Combine and thicken

Add the peppers and sauce and bring to a boil over high heat. Return the eggplant and toss. Add the cornstarch slurry and cook until the sauce coats the eggplant, then serve.

## Tips

- Rinse off the salt after marinating the eggplant, then squeeze out the water thoroughly.
