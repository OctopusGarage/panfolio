# Silky scrambled eggs with shrimp

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 15.-xia-ren-hua-dan / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs and 200 g shrimp
- Scallions to taste, finely chopped
- 2 teaspoons cooking wine and 1.5 teaspoons salt, divided
- 2 teaspoons starch, mixed with water into a slurry
- Cooking oil

## Method

### 1. Marinate the shrimp

Wash and dry the shrimp. Add 2 teaspoons cooking wine and 1 teaspoon salt, then marinate briefly.

### 2. Cook the shrimp

Heat oil and stir-fry the shrimp over high heat until they change color and are cooked through. Remove with their juices and oil and leave to cool.

### 3. Mix the eggs

Beat the eggs with the remaining 0.5 teaspoon salt just until the whites and yolks combine. Do not overbeat.

### 4. Combine with the shrimp

Mix the shrimp, juices, oil and starch slurry into the egg mixture.

### 5. Cook with residual heat

Use a generous amount of oil. Heat until it begins to smoke, turn off the heat, then add the egg mixture. Stir and fold quickly using the residual heat, gently breaking up the edges as they set.

### 6. Serve

Remove as soon as the egg mixture stops flowing. Scatter with scallions.
