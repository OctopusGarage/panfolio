# Scallion-scented cold noodles

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: ali-sauced-noodles / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- A handful of dried noodles
- 1 cucumber
- 1 cilantro sprig
- A few scallions
- Garlic
- 1 spoonful white sesame seeds
- Chili powder
- 1 small spoonful ground Sichuan pepper
- Cooking oil
- Salt, plus 1 spoonful for cooking the noodles
- A little chicken bouillon powder
- A little sugar
- 2 spoonfuls light soy sauce
- Half a spoonful dark soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- A handful of fried peanuts

## Method

### 1. Prepare the vegetables

Crush and mince the garlic. Cut some scallion into small pieces and some into sections, keeping them separate. Cut the cilantro into sections. Slice the cucumber, then cut it into fine strips.

### 2. Release the aromatics

Put the garlic, finely cut scallion, sesame seeds, chili powder and ground Sichuan pepper in a bowl. Heat cooking oil in a pan, fry the scallion sections until fragrant, then remove them. Pour the hot scallion oil over the aromatics in the bowl.

### 3. Mix the dressing

Stir in salt, chicken bouillon powder, sugar, light soy sauce and dark soy sauce. Add sesame oil and mix again.

### 4. Cook and cool the noodles

Heat water until small bubbles appear at the bottom of the pot. Add the salt for cooking the noodles, add the noodles and separate them so they heat evenly. Once the water boils, add cold water. When it boils again, add cold water again. Continue cooking until the noodles are cooked through. Lift them into cooled boiled water, then drain thoroughly.

### 5. Toss and serve

Put the noodles in a large bowl. Add the cucumber, cilantro and dressing, then add the fried peanuts and toss well.

## Tips

- Fry and remove the scallion sections first, then pour the hot oil over the bowl of aromatics to develop both layers of aroma.
- Separating the noodles and adding cold water after the water boils are the demonstrated method for these dried noodles. Cool and drain them before adding the dressing.
