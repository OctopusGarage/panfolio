# Steamed yellow croaker

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 11.-qing-zheng-huang-hua-yu / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- 1 yellow croaker
- 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
- Scallions and ginger, shredded; 2–3 small hot chilies, finely diced
- 2 tablespoons steamed-fish soy sauce and 1 teaspoon cooking wine
- Salt to taste and 1–2 tablespoons hot oil

## Method

### 1. Prepare the fish

Scale and gut the fish and remove the gills. Wash and drain. Score both sides at intervals of about half a centimeter.

### 2. Marinate

Mix the garlic and salt. Rub over the fish and into the cuts. Leave for 20 minutes.

### 3. Prepare to steam

Lay scallion and ginger over the fish and drizzle with cooking wine. Put the steamed-fish soy sauce in a small bowl and steam it alongside the fish.

### 4. Steam the fish

Once the water boils, steam over high heat for 8 minutes, then over low heat for another 2–3 minutes. Remove the fish and discard the used scallion and ginger.

### 5. Finish with hot oil

Add fresh shredded scallion, ginger and finely diced chili. Pour over the hot soy sauce, then drizzle with very hot oil.

## Tips

- The steaming time belongs to this preparation and arrangement. If thickness or quantity changes, do not rely only on the stated minutes. Maintain steam and monitor the water in the pot, checking that the food is cooked through; do not apply this heat sequence to every steamed dish.
