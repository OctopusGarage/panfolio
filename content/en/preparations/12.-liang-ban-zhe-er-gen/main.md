# Cold houttuynia salad

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 12.-liang-ban-zhe-er-gen / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Houttuynia roots, garlic paste, minced ginger and chopped scallions
- Light soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, Sichuan pepper oil and chili oil with chili solids
- Salt to taste

## Method

### 1. Clean the roots

Wash repeatedly to remove dirt and grit. Soak in cold water for 10 minutes, then drain.

### 2. Prepare the aromatics

Mince the ginger, crush the garlic to a paste and chop the scallions.

### 3. Toss with the dressing

Put the roots in a large bowl. Add salt, ginger, garlic paste, light soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, Sichuan pepper oil and chili oil with chili solids. Toss thoroughly.

### 4. Serve

Scatter over the scallions and serve.
