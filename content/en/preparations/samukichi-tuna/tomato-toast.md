# Tuna tomato toast

Japanese chef series · Preparation type: dish

ID: samukichi-tuna / tomato-toast

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Method

### 1. Tuna tomato garlic toast

Ingredients (1 serving): 1 slice bread, 20 g canned tuna, 6 thin tomato slices (about 1/2 medium tomato), 10 g mayonnaise, 1/3 clove grated garlic, a small squeeze of lemon juice and salt; additional garlic for rubbing the bread.

Lightly salt the tomato slices and blot away released moisture with paper towels. Toast the bread, then rub its surface with garlic. Mix the mayonnaise, grated garlic and a little lemon juice into garlic mayo. Arrange the drained tomatoes on the bread, add tuna and drizzle with garlic mayo. Finish with a brief bake in the oven to bring the flavors together.

## Tips

- Do not drain oil-packed tuna excessively: retain some packing oil for richness, aroma and moist texture; use the oil in the fried rice. This advice applies to oil-packed tuna.
- Prolonged cooking makes tuna lose moisture and become dry and crumbly. Cooking it with eggs from the start dries the fish and toughens the eggs; add it when the eggs are softly set.
- Mayonnaise alone can taste heavy. A little lemon juice or vinegar lightens the aftertaste; the toast uses lemon together with tomato acidity.
- Watery tomato slices dilute the toast's flavor. Salt and blot them before assembling.
- Tuna straight from the refrigerator can have a strong, heavy aroma when very cold. Let it warm slightly during preparation to help the flavors blend gently.
