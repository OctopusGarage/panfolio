# Ting’s soy-sauce fried noodles

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: ting-soy-sauce-noodles / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Cooked stir-fry noodles, drained and separated, equivalent to 4 noodle cakes
- Half a jin Chinese chives
- Half a jin trimmed bean sprouts
- Half a red onion
- Dark soy sauce
- Light soy sauce
- Salt
- Cooking oil
- White sesame seeds for garnish

## Method

### 1. Prepare the vegetables

Thinly slice the red onion, cut the Chinese chives into sections and prepare the trimmed bean sprouts. Separate the noodles.

### 2. Cook the vegetables first

Add oil to the wok, add the bean sprouts and separate them with long chopsticks. Add the chives and stir-fry, then add the red onion. Continue tossing, then transfer all the vegetables to a plate.

### 3. Separate and fry the noodles

Add oil to the wok and add the separated noodles. Keep lifting and separating them with long chopsticks while tossing them, until the strands loosen and their surface is dry.

### 4. Season and combine

Add dark soy sauce and lift and toss until the noodles are evenly colored. Return the vegetables to the wok. Keep lifting and mixing, add light soy sauce and salt, then toss well and plate. Garnish with white sesame seeds.

## Tips

- Cook and remove the vegetables first. Return them only after separating and coloring the noodles.
- Lift and separate the strands with long chopsticks to prevent the noodles remaining in a clump.
