# Sausage egg vegetable rice

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: awei-one-pot-rice / sausage-egg

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Each preparation uses rinsed rice and water; other ingredients appear in its method.

## Method

### 1. Sausage, egg and vegetable rice

Cook rice with your usual amount of water for 20 minutes. Arrange carrot, leafy greens, corn and Chinese sausage on top, crack an egg into the center and cook for another 20 minutes. Mix 1.5 spoonfuls light soy sauce, 1 spoonful sesame oil, 0.5 spoonful oyster sauce and 2.5 spoonfuls drinking water; drizzle evenly over the cooked rice.

## Tips

- Ribs can be fried directly or blanched first as needed. Sausage and pork belly release fat, so begin with little oil.
- Choose taro that cooks floury and crumbles under gentle pressure; pan-frying improves aroma. Searing beef also improves aroma, but it can also cook directly with the rice when short of time.
- Coconut milk can replace milk in the curry rice. Keep it slightly moist, or reduce water for a drier result. Meat and vegetables may be varied for each preparation.
- Dried shiitake and meat in these preparations can contribute to umami together, but guanylate associated with dried shiitake and inosinate in meat are different substances. Fresh mushrooms should not be assumed to have the same profile as dried ones; no extra seasoning is required for this explanation.
