# Water spinach with fermented bean curd and chili

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 20.-jiao-si-fu-ru-chao-kong-xin-cai / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Water spinach, garlic and red pepper
- Fermented bean curd and its brine
- 1 teaspoon sugar and 1 teaspoon salt, for blanching
- Salt, starch slurry, cooking oil and peanut oil

## Method

### 1. Prepare the ingredients

Wash and cut the water spinach into lengths. Mince the garlic and shred the pepper. Mash the fermented bean curd with its brine into a paste.

### 2. Blanch the greens

Bring water to a boil. Add a little oil, 1 teaspoon sugar and 1 teaspoon salt. Blanch the water spinach until just cooked, then drain.

### 3. Fry the sauce

Heat oil and fry the garlic, pepper and fermented bean curd paste until fragrant.

### 4. Stir-fry and serve

Add the water spinach and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute. Season with salt, add a little starch slurry to thicken, then finish with a little peanut oil and toss before serving.
