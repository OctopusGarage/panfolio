# Seafood glass noodle casserole

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 08.-hai-xian-fen-si-bao / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Glass noodles and large shrimp
- 1 egg, minced garlic and chopped scallions
- Shredded green pepper, carrot or similar vegetables
- 1/3 spoonful dark soy sauce and 4 spoonfuls light soy sauce, divided
- 1 spoonful oyster sauce, white pepper to taste and cooking oil

## Method

### 1. Prepare the glass noodles

Soak the noodles in boiling water for 20 seconds, then immediately transfer to cold water for 10 minutes. Drain and cut into shorter lengths. Mix with 1/3 spoonful dark soy sauce and 2 spoonfuls light soy sauce.

### 2. Prepare the shrimp and sauce

Remove the shrimp heads and shells, keeping the tails. Slit the backs and remove the veins. Reserve the heads. Mix 2 spoonfuls light soy sauce, 1 spoonful oyster sauce and white pepper for the sauce.

### 3. Make the shrimp oil

Heat oil in a pan. Fry the shrimp heads over low heat until golden and crisp, then remove them, leaving the flavored oil.

### 4. Cook the egg and vegetables

Add garlic to the shrimp oil. Crack in the egg and scramble it. Add the shredded vegetables and cook until nearly cooked.

### 5. Combine noodles and shrimp

Add the noodles and toss with chopsticks for about 1 minute, then add the shrimp meat. Pour in the sauce and stir-fry quickly. Add scallions just before serving.

### 6. Transfer to the clay pot

Transfer the noodles to a preheated clay pot and serve.

## Tips

- Brief hot soaking followed by cold water helps the glass noodles stay springy.
- Loosen the noodles promptly in the pan to prevent clumping.
