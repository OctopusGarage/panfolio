# Smashed cucumber with all-purpose sauce

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 19.-wan-neng-tiao-liao-zhi-pai-huang-gua / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Cucumber, garlic, small hot chilies, cilantro, toasted sesame seeds and peanuts
- 1 spoonful ground dried chili and 1 large spoonful hot oil
- 2 spoonfuls light soy sauce, 1 spoonful oyster sauce and 2 spoonfuls Chinese aged vinegar
- Half a spoonful salt, a little sugar and sesame oil

## Method

### 1. Prepare the ingredients

Wash and smash the cucumber, then cut into chunks. Mince the garlic, slice the chilies and cut the cilantro into lengths.

### 2. Make the chili oil

Put the garlic, chilies, sesame seeds and ground dried chili in a bowl. Pour over hot oil to release the aroma.

### 3. Mix the dressing

Add the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, vinegar, salt, sugar and sesame oil. Stir well.

### 4. Toss and serve

Add the dressing, cilantro and peanuts to the cucumber. Toss thoroughly and serve.
