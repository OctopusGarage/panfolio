# Pan-seared yin xue yu

Chinese · Preparation type: dish

ID: 18.-xiang-jian-yin-xue-yu / main

Use the selected ingredients and method below. Shared tips apply only when their food and conditions match this preparation.

## Ingredients

- Yin xue yu fish and butter
- Scallions, ginger and garlic
- Oyster sauce, light soy sauce and a little water

## Method

### 1. Prepare the ingredients

Cut scallions into lengths and slice the ginger and garlic. Pat the fish dry and make a cut in the middle of the skin to reduce curling.

### 2. Sear the fish

Melt butter in a hot pan. Add the fish and sear until golden on both sides.

### 3. Add the sauce

Mix oyster sauce, light soy sauce and a little water and pour into the pan. Add the scallion, ginger and garlic.

### 4. Reduce and serve

Cook over low heat until the fish is cooked through and the sauce has reduced, then serve.
