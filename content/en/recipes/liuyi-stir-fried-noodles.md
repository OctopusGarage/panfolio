# LiuYi’s home-style stir-fried noodles

Chinese · ID: liuyi-stir-fried-noodles

## Choose a preparation

- [LiuYi’s home-style stir-fried noodles](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/liuyi-stir-fried-noodles/main.md)

## Ingredients

- Fresh alkaline noodles, leafy greens, onion, carrot, ham sausage, eggs and garlic
- White vinegar, vegetable oil, rapeseed oil and dark soy sauce
- Mala seasoning blend about 5 g; light soy sauce about 10 g
- Salt, chicken seasoning, pepper and sesame oil

## Method

### 1. Prepare

Slice thick greens finely and shred the onion. Cut the carrot diagonally into slightly thick slices, then fine strips; cut the sausage into thick slices, then strips. Crush and mince a generous amount of garlic. Beat the eggs with a little white vinegar.

### 2. Parboil and coat the noodles

Add a little vegetable oil to water, bring to a boil and add fresh noodles over high heat. Stir to heat evenly. After boiling resumes, add cold water in two or three additions; cook for about 40 seconds until partly cooked, roughly seven-tenths done. Transfer to cold water, stir and let cool. Drain very thoroughly. Gently toss with a generous coating of vegetable oil and dark soy sauce to color evenly.

### 3. Cook the eggs

Dry the pan over heat and coat thoroughly with a generous amount of vegetable oil. Add the eggs and push gently until set and lightly golden on the surface; remove.

### 4. Stir-fry the vegetables and noodles

Heat rapeseed oil until fragrant. Fry half the garlic, then add carrot, onion and sausage for about 10 seconds. Add greens and fry until just softened, about 5～6 seconds. Add noodles and toss; mix in mala seasoning, light soy sauce, a little salt, chicken seasoning and pepper.

### 5. Finish over high heat

Return the eggs and add the remaining garlic. Toss several times over high heat until fragrant. Add a little sesame oil, toss briefly and turn off the heat to serve.

## Tips

- Small tender greens also work; finely cutting thicker greens helps them cook and take seasoning. Onion and the final garlic addition supply aroma.
- Do not overboil the noodles. Cooling, draining and coating with oil and dark soy sauce before frying reduce sticking and distribute color. Cook the eggs slightly firmer and lightly golden for this stir-fry.
