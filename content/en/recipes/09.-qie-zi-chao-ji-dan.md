# Eggplant and scrambled eggs

Chinese · ID: 09.-qie-zi-chao-ji-dan

## Choose a preparation

- [Eggplant and scrambled eggs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/09.-qie-zi-chao-ji-dan/main.md)

## Ingredients

- 2 eggplants and 2 eggs
- Garlic, ginger, small hot chilies, green pepper and tomato
- Salt, chicken bouillon seasoning, pepper, oyster sauce and light soy sauce
- Cornstarch, water and cooking oil

## Method

### 1. Prepare the eggplant

Remove the stems and cut the eggplant into cubes about 1 cm wide. Rub with salt and leave for 20 minutes. Rinse 2 times and drain thoroughly.

### 2. Prepare the additions and sauce

Mince the garlic and ginger, slice the chilies into rings, and dice the pepper and tomato. Mix salt, chicken seasoning, pepper, oyster sauce, light soy sauce, cornstarch and water into a sauce.

### 3. Cook the eggs

Beat the eggs with pepper, stirring in one direction. Heat a little oil and cook the eggs over low heat until they form pieces, then remove.

### 4. Cook the eggplant

Add a little oil and slowly pan-fry and stir-fry the eggplant until cooked. Remove and set aside.

### 5. Cook the vegetables

Add oil and fry the garlic, ginger and chilies until fragrant. Add the tomato and cook until juicy, then add the green pepper and stir-fry quickly.

### 6. Combine and serve

Return the eggs and eggplant. Add the sauce and stir-fry over high heat until the ingredients are evenly coated, then serve.
