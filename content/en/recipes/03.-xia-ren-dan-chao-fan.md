# Shrimp and egg fried rice

Chinese · ID: 03.-xia-ren-dan-chao-fan

## Choose a preparation

- [Shrimp and egg fried rice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/03.-xia-ren-dan-chao-fan/main.md)

## Ingredients

- Shell-on shrimp; separate the meat and shells
- Cold cooked rice, eggs and scallions
- Salt, cooking oil and water

## Method

### 1. Make the shrimp broth

Stir-fry the shrimp shells until fully cooked and fragrant. Crush them, add water and simmer to make a broth, then strain. Reserve the shrimp oil for frying.

### 2. Cook the shrimp

Heat oil in a pan. Add the shrimp meat with a little water and shrimp oil, and stir-fry until fragrant. Alternatively, skip this separate step and cook the shrimp with the eggs.

### 3. Fry the eggs and rice

In another pan, heat a little shrimp oil. Fry the scallions, then add the eggs and cold rice. Break up the rice with a spatula.

### 4. Add broth gradually

Add the shrimp broth in small additions, stir-frying as you go so the rice absorbs the liquid. Do not pour in too much at once.

### 5. Season and serve

Add salt and shrimp juices. Continue stir-frying until the grains are dry. Finish with a little shrimp oil, toss and serve.

## Tips

- Add the shrimp broth gradually to avoid wet, clumped rice.
- Keep the grains separate without frying them excessively dry; let the rice absorb the flavor of the shrimp broth.
