# Soft Egg Crepe Wraps

## Choose a preparation

- [Soft Egg Crepe Wraps](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/soft-egg-crepe-wraps/main.md)

## Ingredients

- 1 spoonful of all-purpose or bread flour, measured with a soup ladle
- 2 spoonfuls of water, measured with the same ladle, plus water for rinsing the potatoes
- A little salt
- Eggs: 1 for the batter, plus 1 per crepe
- Cooking oil and black sesame seeds
- Filling: potatoes, scallion segments, salt, MSG, lettuce, and a sauce you like, such as ketchup

## Method

### 1. Mix the batter

Measure the flour and water with the same soup ladle and add a little salt. Stir in one direction with 3 chopsticks for about 1 minute, until the rough batter becomes smooth like yogurt. Crack in the batter egg and stir until combined. This batch makes about 3 crepes.

### 2. Prepare the potato shreds

Cut the potatoes into fine shreds. Rinse off the surface starch with water and drain well. Rinsing helps the shreds stay crisp when stir-fried.

### 3. Spread the batter thin and flip

Preheat a flat skillet over medium heat. If the surface has no oil, wipe on a thin film. Pour in 1 spoonful of batter and tilt the skillet to spread it into a thin layer. Turn the heat down to low once the batter is in. When the surface changes color and sets, spray on a little oil. Use a spatula to flip the crepe when the rim starts to lift.

### 4. Spread the egg over the crepe and finish cooking

Crack 1 egg onto the flipped crepe, spread it with a spatula, and scatter black sesame seeds over it. Wait until the egg surface sets, spray on a little oil, then flip it egg-side down. Take the crepe out when it visibly puffs and the egg side is cooked. Repeat with the remaining batter.

### 5. Stir-fry the filling and assemble

Add a little oil to the skillet. Fry some scallion segments until fragrant, then stir-fry the drained potato shreds with a little salt and MSG. When the potatoes are cooked, stir in more scallion segments and take them out of the pan. Spread your chosen sauce on the crepe, add 2 lettuce leaves and the cooked potato shreds, then fold or roll it up.

## Tips

- Measure the flour and water with the same ladle to keep the ratio. Tilt the skillet as soon as the batter goes in. Once it begins to set, it is harder to spread evenly.
- For a meat or seafood filling, add cooked chicken cutlet, bacon, pan-fried shrimp, or pork floss.
