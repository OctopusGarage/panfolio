## Method

### 1. Beef-ball noodle soup

1. Put frozen beef balls straight into cold water and bring to a boil.
2. Add the instant noodles and seasoning powder.
3. When the noodles are nearly cooked, add two leafy greens. Add the scallion oil sachet and light soy sauce before serving.

Tip: The beef-ball cooking liquid becomes the noodle broth.

### 2. Shacha, peanut and milk noodles

1. Fry 2 spoonfuls shacha sauce until fragrant.
2. Add boiling water, 1 small spoonful peanut butter and half a cup of milk.
3. Add the noodles and cook through. Add only a little seasoning powder to taste.

Tip: The shacha sauce, peanut butter and milk make a rich broth.

### 3. Cantonese ginger-scallion tossed noodles

1. Put shredded ginger and chopped scallions in a bowl and pour hot peanut oil over them.
2. Mix in chicken-flavored instant-noodle seasoning powder to make a sauce.
3. Cook and drain the noodles, then toss with the sauce. Any remaining seasoning powder can be dissolved in hot water for soup.

Tip: Prepare the ginger-scallion oil before tossing the noodles.

### 4. Egg custard with noodle broth

1. Beat the egg and mix in a little instant-noodle broth.
2. Skim off the bubbles and microwave on low power for 1 minute 30 seconds.
3. Drizzle with a little sesame oil and soy sauce.

Tip: Heating varies with microwave power and the amount of egg mixture; check that the egg has set.

### 5. Fried egg and ham spicy noodles

1. Pan-fry the egg and ham sausage until lightly browned and fragrant.
2. Add water and bring to a boil. Add the noodles and seasoning sachets and cook through.

Tip: Frying first adds egg and ham flavor to the broth.

### 6. Swirled poached-egg noodles

1. Bring water to a boil and stir in the seasoning powder.
2. Lower the heat, stir a vortex into the water and crack in the egg.
3. Wait for the center of the egg to set, then add the noodles and cook through.

Tip: Do not stir the egg before it has set.

### 7. Salted egg yolk and ham noodle soup

1. Heat oil, fry the ginger until fragrant, then add salted egg yolk and cook until grainy.
2. Add ham sausage, hot water and the noodles.
3. Add vegetables such as Chinese cabbage or tofu if desired and cook through.

Tip: Salted egg yolk is already salty; use the seasoning powder sparingly.

### 8. Century egg and cilantro noodles

1. Fry ginger, century egg and finely chopped ham sausage until fragrant.
2. Add boiling water and seasoning powder, then cook the noodles in the broth.
3. Scatter with cilantro at the end.

Tip: Add the cilantro just before serving.

### 9. Curry and potato noodles

1. Pan-fry the potatoes until cooked through, then add a curry block and fry until fragrant.
2. Add hot water and cook the noodles in the broth.

Tip: Curry blocks are already seasoned, so the powder sachet can be omitted.

### 10. Tomato stir-fried instant noodles

1. Peel and finely chop the tomatoes. Fry garlic until fragrant, then add the tomatoes and cook until juicy.
2. Add tomato-flavored noodle seasoning and hot water.
3. Add egg and noodles and cook, stirring, until done.

Tip: For a stronger-flavored variation, stir-fry fermented-black-bean chili sauce with tofu or cured sausage together with the noodles.
