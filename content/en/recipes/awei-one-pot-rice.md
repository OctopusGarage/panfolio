## Ingredients

- Each preparation uses rinsed rice and water; other ingredients appear in its method.

## Method

### 1. Chestnut and pork rib rice

Heat oil with ginger slices and scallion sections. Add washed pork ribs and fry until they begin to release fat; pour Shaoxing wine around the pan edge. Add fresh shiitake, carrot, chestnuts and water. Over low heat season with a small spoonful of salt, white pepper, oyster sauce and light soy sauce; cover and bring to a boil. Pour everything over rinsed rice in the rice cooker, using your normal rice-cooking water amount as a guide. Cook on the rice setting for 40 minutes and finish with chopped scallions. Add a few drops of dark soy sauce if a deeper color is desired.

### 2. Chinese sausage and potato rice

Use a little oil to fry ginger, scallion sections and Cantonese sausage until fragrant and the sausage releases fat. Add corn, peas, carrot and potato. Season with a spoonful of salt, white pepper and oyster sauce, without light soy sauce. Add water, cover and boil for 1 minute. Pour over rinsed rice, distribute evenly with chopsticks and cook on the rice setting for 40 minutes. Remove ginger and finish with scallions.

### 3. Taro and chestnut rice

Fry skinless pork belly gently over low heat with a little oil until fragrant and releasing fat. Pour Shaoxing wine around the edge; add ginger, scallion sections and a little dark soy sauce. Pan-fry taro, then add dried shiitake, chestnuts and krill or dried tiny shrimp. Once fragrant, add 2 bowls of water, salt, a scant half spoonful of sugar, oyster sauce and pepper. Boil for 1 minute, pour over rinsed rice and press the taro under the liquid. Cook on the rice setting for 40 minutes. Remove ginger and scallion sections, then add chopped scallions and mix.

### 4. Pumpkin and beef rice

Cut steak into strips and mix with black-pepper lemon sea salt, cornstarch and a little oil. Sear in a little hot oil; add ginger, scallion sections, dried shiitake, carrot, peas and small pumpkin, and stir-fry. Add 2 bowls of water, cover and bring to a boil; season with a small spoonful of salt and oyster sauce. Pour over rinsed rice and press ingredients down with chopsticks. Cook on the rice setting for 40 minutes and remove ginger and scallion sections.

### 5. Sausage, egg and vegetable rice

Cook rice with your usual amount of water for 20 minutes. Arrange carrot, leafy greens, corn and Chinese sausage on top, crack an egg into the center and cook for another 20 minutes. Mix 1.5 spoonfuls light soy sauce, 1 spoonful sesame oil, 0.5 spoonful oyster sauce and 2.5 spoonfuls drinking water; drizzle evenly over the cooked rice.

### 6. Curry chicken rice

Dice boneless chicken thighs and mix with salt, white pepper, Shaoxing wine, ginger, scallions and a little light soy sauce. Mix in 2 spoonfuls cornstarch, coat with a little oil and marinate for 5 minutes. Brown in hot oil, fry onion until fragrant, then add carrot, potato and fresh shiitake. Add 1 bowl milk, 1 bowl water and 3 pieces curry roux; cover and cook for 2 minutes. Once boiling, pour over rinsed rice, press ingredients down and cook on the rice setting for 40 minutes.

## Tips

- Ribs can be fried directly or blanched first as needed. Sausage and pork belly release fat, so begin with little oil.
- Choose taro that cooks floury and crumbles under gentle pressure; pan-frying improves aroma. Searing beef also improves aroma, but it can also cook directly with the rice when short of time.
- Coconut milk can replace milk in the curry rice. Keep it slightly moist, or reduce water for a drier result. Meat and vegetables may be varied for each preparation.
- Dried shiitake and meat in these preparations can contribute to umami together, but guanylate associated with dried shiitake and inosinate in meat are different substances. Fresh mushrooms should not be assumed to have the same profile as dried ones; no extra seasoning is required for this explanation.
