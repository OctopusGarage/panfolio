# Spinach and scrambled eggs

Chinese · ID: 14.-bo-cai-chao-ji-dan

## Choose a preparation

- [Spinach and scrambled eggs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/14.-bo-cai-chao-ji-dan/main.md)

## Ingredients

- 1 bunch spinach and 4 eggs
- Scallions to taste, minced
- Half a spoonful salt and cooking oil as needed

## Method

### 1. Blanch the spinach

Bring water to a boil with a little salt. Blanch the spinach until bright green, then transfer to cold water. Once cool, squeeze dry and cut into lengths.

### 2. Prepare the eggs

Beat the eggs and mince the scallions.

### 3. Scramble the eggs

Heat oil until very hot. Add the eggs, cook through and remove.

### 4. Fry the spinach

Turn the same pan to low heat. Fry the scallions until fragrant, then add the spinach and cook until it releases a little moisture.

### 5. Combine and season

Return the eggs, add salt and toss quickly over high heat, then serve.
