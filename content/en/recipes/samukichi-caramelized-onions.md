# Deep caramelized onions and rich onion soup

- [Rich onion soup](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/samukichi-caramelized-onions/soup.md)

## Ingredients

Caramelized onions: onions, 5 ml olive oil (1 teaspoon), a pinch of salt and a little water.

Onion soup (1 serving): 5 ml olive oil, 1 clove garlic, 400 ml water, 5 g chicken bouillon powder, caramelized onions made from 1/2 onion and salt. Optional additions: cooked chicken breast, asparagus and black pepper.

## Method

### 1. Slice evenly

Slice onions evenly, about 2～3 mm thick, to reduce uneven cooking from differences in thickness.

### 2. Cook over medium-low heat

Set the pan over medium-low heat. Add 5 ml olive oil (1 teaspoon), add the onions and coat evenly. Add a pinch of salt to help draw out moisture, then cook slowly over medium-low heat.

### 3. Let the onions contact the pan

Let the onions cook against the pan for a while before stirring, repeating this cycle rather than stirring constantly. They begin to turn translucent after about 10～15 minutes.

### 4. Deglaze the browned bits

When browned bits form on the pan bottom, add a little water to loosen them and mix them back into the onions. Repeat as more browned bits develop, preventing them from burning. A deep caramelized color begins to develop after about 20～30 minutes.

### 5. Finish deep and soft

Continue over medium-low heat until deeply caramelized, soft and cohesive, about 30～40 minutes. Judge by color and texture rather than stopping at light brown.

### 6. Make onion soup

Slice the garlic and warm it slowly with the soup's olive oil over low heat until fragrant without browning. Add the water and bouillon powder and simmer for a few minutes to infuse the garlic aroma. Taste and add salt if needed. Turn off the heat, then gently mix in the caramelized onions. Warm them through with residual heat to preserve texture and aroma. Finish with black pepper; add cooked chicken breast and asparagus if desired.

### 7. Store a batch for later

Put extra caramelized onions in a resealable storage bag, flatten into a thin layer and seal. This makes it easy to take the amount needed for soups, curries and sauces. Refrigerate for 3～4 days or freeze for about 2～3 months.

## Tips

- High heat can burn the surface before moisture evaporates and sweetness develops, making it bitter. Medium-low heat and even slices give better control.
- Constant stirring lowers the temperature and makes browning harder. Pause between stirring so the onions have time against the pan.
- Browned pan deposits add rich flavor; dissolve them promptly with a little water. Blackened, bitter residues differ from properly browned bits so prevent the deposits from burning that far.
- Light brown is an intermediate stage for these deeply caramelized onions. A deep color and soft, cohesive texture signal completion; heat level and onion quantity affect the time. Onions intended for soup can retain a little texture.
