# Egg-fried rice vermicelli

Chinese · ID: 04.-ji-dan-chao-mi-fen

## Choose a preparation

- [Egg-fried rice vermicelli](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/04.-ji-dan-chao-mi-fen/main.md)

## Ingredients

- Rice vermicelli; the original method uses Alijia fragrant rice vermicelli
- 1 egg
- Bean sprouts, scallion whites and greens, and congtou (aromatic allium bulbs)
- Salt, light soy sauce and cooking oil

## Method

### 1. Briefly boil the noodles

Bring water to a boil with a little oil and salt. Add the rice vermicelli and loosen it. Cook for about 2 minutes; drain when the water returns to a boil.

### 2. Rest under a lid

Drain the noodles into a bowl. Cover for 3 minutes, turn them over, then cover for another 3 minutes.

### 3. Prepare the other ingredients

Wash and drain the bean sprouts. Cut the scallions into lengths and split the congtou bulbs. Beat the egg with salt.

### 4. Cook the egg

Heat oil in a pan. Add the egg and cook until just set, then remove.

### 5. Fry the noodles

Add oil to the same pan. Spread out the noodles, let them fry briefly, then turn them over. Use chopsticks to loosen them and fry until fragrant and lightly toasted.

### 6. Combine and season

Add the egg, scallion whites and bean sprouts in that order. Season with light soy sauce. Add the scallion greens, turn off the heat and toss before serving.

## Tips

- After resting, the noodles should be springy and relatively dry, rather than wet and sticky.
- Use a spatula together with chopsticks to loosen the noodles.
