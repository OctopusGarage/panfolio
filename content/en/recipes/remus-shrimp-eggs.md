# Remus’s silky eggs with shrimp

Chinese · ID: remus-shrimp-eggs

## Choose a preparation

- [Remus’s silky eggs with shrimp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/remus-shrimp-eggs/main.md)

## Ingredients

- 14 fresh shrimp
- 6 eggs
- 1 scallion
- Salt
- Ground pepper
- Starch
- Sesame oil
- A little light soy sauce
- Cooking oil

## Method

### 1. Marinate the shrimp

Peel the shrimp. Add salt, ground pepper, starch and sesame oil, and mix by hand. Finely slice the scallion.

### 2. Handle the eggs separately

Separate the yolks and whites into different bowls. Mix a little light soy sauce into the yolks. Keep the whites separate for now rather than combining everything at the start.

### 3. Cook and drain the shrimp

Add oil to the wok and stir-fry the shrimp until nearly cooked through. Transfer them to a strainer to drain off oil and liquid. Cooked shrimp release some liquid, so drain them before adding them to the eggs.

### 4. Combine the egg mixture

Add more oil to the wok. Add the scallion to the yolks, then pour in the whites and stir briefly. Add the drained shrimp. Have a serving plate ready.

### 5. Quickly cook the eggs

Quickly pour the shrimp and egg mixture into the wok. Push and fold it with a spatula. Once the eggs begin setting, turn off the heat and keep folding so the remaining heat cooks the rest of the egg. Plate and eat immediately.

## Tips

- Mix the yolks first and add the whites only for the final brief mixing. Keep this order rather than fully combining both at the beginning.
- Drain the shrimp after stir-frying so their oil and liquid do not enter the eggs.
- Eggs absorb oil readily, so replenish the wok oil before cooking them. Finish setting the eggs with residual heat after turning off the heat.
- Eat as soon as the dish is ready; standing causes liquid to collect and affects the texture.
