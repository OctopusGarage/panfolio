# Ali’s layered scallion meat pancakes

Chinese · ID: ali-scallion-meat-pancakes

## Choose a preparation

- [Ali’s layered scallion meat pancakes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/ali-scallion-meat-pancakes/main.md)

## Ingredients

- Makes 5. Dough: 500 g all-purpose flour, 250 g boiling water, 100 g room-temperature water and 30 g oil.
- Paste: 100 g oil, 70 g all-purpose flour, 3 g salt, 2 g ground Sichuan pepper and 2 g five-spice powder; 200 g scallions in total, whites for frying and greens chopped.
- Filling: 350 g ground pork or beef, 5 g minced ginger, 5～6 g salt, 3 g white pepper and the fried scallion whites; sesame seeds and oil for frying.

## Method

### 1. Scald part of the flour and rest

Divide flour into two portions. Stir boiling water into one half, then gradually incorporate the other half. Add room-temperature water and oil, knead for 3～5 minutes, rest for 20 minutes and knead for another 3～5 minutes until smooth. Divide into 5 balls, place in an oiled container, brush with oil and rest for 60 minutes.

### 2. Make scallion oil paste and filling

Separate scallion whites and greens; chop greens. Combine the paste flour, Sichuan pepper, five-spice and salt. Heat oil and fry whites until golden; reserve them for the filling. Pour hot scallion oil into the paste ingredients and mix. Combine meat with ginger, salt, white pepper and the fried whites.

### 3. Stretch and fill

Oil the board lightly and slowly stretch each rested dough ball thin by hand. Spread with paste, add a thin layer of meat and sprinkle with scallion greens. Roll up along the long side to enclose the filling, coil the log from one end into a round, then press into a thick pancake, sprinkle with sesame and rest for 15 minutes.

### 4. Flatten and fry

Press by hand on baking paper to about 0.5 cm thick. Add a little oil to a frying pan, or omit it in a nonstick pan. Fry over medium heat, turning every 1～2 minutes, for about 5～6 minutes until cooked.

## Tips

- Well-rested dough stretches readily; stretching it thin creates more layers. Small tears exposing filling during shaping are normal.
- Dough and filling can be prepared the night before and refrigerated. The flattened uncooked pancakes may be frozen for later frying.
