# Feng’s beef rice vermicelli

Chinese · ID: feng-night-market-vermicelli

## Choose a preparation

- [Feng’s beef rice vermicelli](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/feng-night-market-vermicelli/main.md)

## Ingredients

- Hsinchu rice vermicelli and diaolong beef
- Half a cabbage, half a carrot, a small portion of onion, bean sprouts and scallions
- 2 eggs, cooking oil and lard
- Matsutake seasoning, cooking wine, light soy sauce and dark soy sauce
- Salt one large spoonful and oyster sauce half a spoonful; a little extra salt for the eggs
- Chili oil, optional at the table

## Method

### 1. Soak noodles and cut vegetables

Soak vermicelli in warm water for 10 minutes. Shred and wash the cabbage; shred carrot and onion and wash sprouts. Separate scallion whites from the remaining sections.

### 2. Marinate beef and drain noodles

Slice beef, then cut into thin strips. Mix with matsutake seasoning and a little cooking wine and soy sauce to marinate. Rinse the soaked noodles in cold water, squeeze out water and toss with cooking oil to prevent clumping. Beat eggs with a little salt.

### 3. Cook eggs and beef

Heat an iron wok with oil, add eggs and quickly scramble; remove. Melt lard in the wok and fry scallion whites until fragrant. Add beef and fry until its color changes, then add onion and fry until fragrant.

### 4. Toss the vermicelli

Add cabbage, carrot and sprouts and fry until vegetables and sprouts soften. Add vermicelli and salt, pour light soy sauce around the wok edge and add a little dark soy sauce for color and the oyster sauce. Return eggs. Toss evenly over high heat using chopsticks, then mix in scallion sections. Serve with optional chili oil.

## Tips

- Home burners have limited power, so squeeze the noodles dry to reduce sticking; chopsticks help mix them evenly.
- Cabbage supplies a crisp texture that small bok choy does not match. Pork foreleg meat, ham sausage or shrimp may replace beef to taste.
