# Microwave Chocolate Milk Tea

Drinks · ID: microwave-chocolate-milk-tea

## Choose a preparation

- [Microwave Chocolate Milk Tea](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/microwave-chocolate-milk-tea/main.md)

## Ingredients

- Loose-leaf black tea
- Chocolate, broken into small pieces
- A little warm water
- Milk

## Method

### 1. Mix the tea and chocolate

Put the tea leaves and chocolate pieces in a microwave-safe mug. Add a little warm water and stir to wet both the tea and the chocolate.

### 2. Heat the tea base on high

Remove the spoon and microwave the mug on high for 2 minutes to melt the chocolate and draw out the tea. The mixture should be dark brown when you take it out.

### 3. Add the milk and heat again

Pour in the milk and stir to combine it with the chocolate-tea base at the bottom of the mug. Remove the spoon and microwave on high for 1 minute. Stir after heating. The milk tea should be an even cocoa brown.

## Tips

- Leave some room at the top of the mug for the milk to rise. High power varies by microwave. Watch the surface and stop to check if it starts to rise.
- For a smoother drink, strain out the loose tea leaves through a fine sieve before drinking.
