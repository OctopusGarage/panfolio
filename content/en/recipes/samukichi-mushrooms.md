# Golden mushrooms with garlic, butter and soy

Japanese chef series · ID: samukichi-mushrooms

## Choose a preparation

- [Golden mushrooms with garlic, butter and soy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/samukichi-mushrooms/main.md)

## Ingredients

- Large white button mushrooms, 3 (serves 1–2)
- Water, 10 ml
- Butter, 10 g
- Garlic, 1 clove, chopped
- Soy sauce, 5 g
- A little olive oil
- A little salt
- A little lemon juice
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method

### 1. Wash briefly, dry and cut thickly

Quickly wash the mushrooms to remove surface dirt without soaking for a long time. If only lightly dirty, wipe with a damp paper towel instead. Pat the surface thoroughly dry and cut into quarters, keeping the pieces thick so they remain plump after cooking.

### 2. Start with a little water and no oil

Put the mushrooms in a pan without oil. Add 10 ml water and cook over high heat, allowing them to gradually release their own moisture.

### 3. Evaporate the moisture before adding oil

Continue heating to evaporate the liquid. Watch for moisture almost disappearing and the sound changing from simmering to sizzling. Once the liquid has cooked off, add a little olive oil and reduce to medium-high heat.

### 4. Leave to brown

Spread out the mushrooms and leave undisturbed rather than stirring constantly. Turn once the contact surface is deeply golden. When both sides are well browned, season lightly with salt.

### 5. Lower the heat and infuse garlic butter

Reduce to medium-low heat. Move the mushrooms to the upper side of the pan and tilt it. Add the butter and garlic in the lower exposed area, allowing the garlic to infuse the melted butter gently. Once fragrant, toss to coat all the mushrooms.

### 6. Sizzle the soy sauce and finish off the heat

Add 5 g soy sauce to the hot pan surface, let it sizzle briefly, then toss with the mushrooms. Once coated with the aroma, turn off the heat. Plate, squeeze over a little lemon juice and finish with black pepper. The mushrooms should be golden outside and plump and juicy inside.

## Tips

- Drying after a quick wash and adding a little water in the pan serve different stages: the first removes excess surface water, while the second starts moisture release. Release moisture, cook it off and only then brown.
- A large amount of oil at the start can be absorbed, leading to excess oil use. Adding oil while liquid remains also makes good browning harder.
- Constant stirring reduces contact with the pan and prevents deep golden browning. Spread out, leave undisturbed and turn after browning.
- Garlic and butter add finishing aroma rather than doing the prolonged browning. Added too early, garlic can burn and butter can lose its fragrance. Brown the mushrooms first, then lower the heat before adding them.
- Let soy sauce briefly contact the hot pan for aroma before tossing. Lemon and black pepper go on after plating to brighten and balance the rich butter.
