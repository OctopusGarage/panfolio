# Garlic and cumin ribs

Chinese · ID: 16.-suan-xiang-zi-ran-pai-gu

## Choose a preparation

- [Garlic and cumin ribs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/16.-suan-xiang-zi-ran-pai-gu/main.md)

## Ingredients

- Ribs as needed and 1 onion, finely chopped
- Minced garlic, small hot chilies and scallion lengths as needed
- Ginger and cooking wine, for boiling the ribs
- Chili powder, ground cumin and barbecue seasoning to taste
- 2 spoonfuls light soy sauce, a little salt and sugar
- Cooking oil

## Method

### 1. Boil the ribs

Boil the ribs with scallion, ginger and cooking wine until mostly cooked, then drain.

### 2. Fry the ribs

Heat oil and fry the ribs until golden and crisp on the outside, then remove.

### 3. Fry the aromatics

Leave a little oil in the pan. Fry the garlic, onion and chilies until fragrant.

### 4. Add the spices

Add chili powder, cumin and barbecue seasoning, then stir to combine.

### 5. Season the ribs

Return the ribs. Add 2 spoonfuls light soy sauce and a little salt and sugar, then toss until flavored.

### 6. Serve

Add the scallion lengths and toss quickly before serving.
