# Rosalina’s tomato beef stew with pasta

Chinese · ID: rosalina-tomato-beef

## Choose a preparation

- [Rosalina’s tomato beef stew with pasta](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OctopusGarage/panfolio/main/content/en/preparations/rosalina-tomato-beef/main.md)

## Ingredients

- About 750 g trimmed beef rib fingers; 2～3 tomatoes; 1 large onion; 1 garlic shoot (optional); about 35 g minced garlic.
- 340 g tomato pasta sauce; half a jar water; 7～10 g smoked paprika; 30 ml Worcestershire sauce (optional); salt, black pepper, white pepper, mixed herbs, chili powder and olive oil; pasta.

## Method

### 1. Prepare ingredients

Finely dice onion, mince garlic, slice the white part of the garlic shoot and dice tomatoes, peeled or unpeeled. Wash and dry beef, trim excess fat while retaining some, cut into pieces and mix with salt, black pepper, a little white pepper and mixed herbs.

### 2. Render fat and brown in batches

Start the trimmed fat in a cold pot and render slowly over medium heat, avoiding high heat. Reserve half the fat; brown beef in batches in the remaining fat until both sides color, then remove.

### 3. Build the aromatic base

Return reserved beef fat, adding olive oil if needed. Fry onion until translucent, then garlic shoot whites and minced garlic until fragrant. Return beef with its juices. Fry with smoked paprika, optional chili powder, salt, black pepper and mixed herbs, then stir in tomatoes.

### 4. Simmer and serve

Add tomato pasta sauce. Shake half a jar of water in the empty jar and pour it in; add optional Worcestershire sauce. Taste for salt, cover and simmer on the lowest heat for 60 minutes. Taste again and check that the beef is tender. Serve alone or with pasta, pouring the sauce over it so it absorbs the flavor.

## Tips

- Finely chopped onion melts into the sauce more readily. Avoid adding all the beef at once: batches limit the temperature drop and allow even browning.
- Some rib fingers may be replaced with shank, but the creator advises against replacing all of them because some fat contributes flavor. Tomato sauce provides the base without stock or red wine; fresh tomatoes are also optional.
